About the Project

"The process of colonization is complex and is never fully realized. It requires the constant imposition of a foreign culture on the colonized, and that this culture have a hierarchical structure where the colonized are always symbolically below the colonizer. This is what the psychiatrist from Martinique, Frantz Fanon, diagnoses as a logic of the colonial condition in the early 1950s in his book entitled Black Skin, White Masks. Fanon describes how colonial regimes shape public institutions and the social environment in accordance with the learning of colonial logic. So it is not only the colonizers who impose their culture, but also the colonized subjects themselves who, by internalizing colonial logic, despise each other. For Fanon, the West Indian “gradually assimilates the prejudices, myths, and folklore that come from Europe” to eventually become “a slave to this cultural imposition” and “realize that he has been living a lie” (pg. 168-169, Fanon)."
