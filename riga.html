Oil painting using color and texture from the film "Koyaanisqatsi" and the subject and composition from "Stalker".

About the Project

This project explores the intersection of visual art and cinema, drawing inspiration from the films "Koyaanisqatsi" and "Stalker" to create a dialogue between the two mediums. The artwork emphasizes the distortion of subjectivity through modern technology, using color and texture to evoke emotional and psychological responses.

This work investigates the contrasts between the socio-economic outputs of modernized civilizations, represented by overwhelming technological innovation in the experimental film from the 80's Koyanosqaatsi, and the more individual aspects and consequences of modernity, represented by the hopeless search for meaning in a ruined world by the artist, scientist and believer, presented by Tarkovsky's film, Stalker. For this comparison, I juxtaposed stills from the film to create a new composition that shifts the perspective of each film into the other. Here previously disconnected realities, now combined, point towards trends in their spatial organization that denote themes in society's psychological state after the first two decades of the 21st century.
