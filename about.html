About Me

Hi, I’m Diego Rodríguez Langevin, an interdisciplinary researcher with a love for designing, managing and communicating complex projects in both art and science. My academic focus is two-pronged. On the one hand I specialize in molecular neuroscience, and have experience in both experimental and computational work. On the other hand I study philosophy with an emphasis on 20th century thinkers and decolonial theory. Creatively I dabble in poetry and graphic design, where I have developed a style based on free association and the juxtaposition of images. My goal is to combine my eclectic set of skills to help develop and realize interdisciplinary projects across academic, civic and creative communities alike.
