About the Project

This project investigates how schizophrenia associated variations in noncoding regions of the CACNA1C gene, as determined through Genome Wide Association Studies (GWAS), influence splicing patterns of this important gene encoding the pore subunit of the L-type calcium channel.

During my rotation in the lab of Dr. Richard Tsien at the NYU Langone Health Neuroscience Institute, I cross-referenced non-coding regions associated with schizophrenia with known regulatory domains in online databases (UCSC genome browser, RBPmap, Ensembl) and ran real-time PCR on cDNA samples from human iPSC-derived neurons that had these regions deleted using CRISPR technology.
