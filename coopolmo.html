About the Project

Our project involves creating a worker-owned cooperative focused on cultural work and artistic production. We aim to build a work and community ecosystem in Puerto Rico that fosters artistic creation and cultural innovation.

CoopOlmo's mission is to create a sustainable and sensible creative ecosystem that produces tangible works based on the ideas of its artists and audiences, taking firm steps toward a robust Puerto Rican cultural management system. What began as a band, Los Olmos, has evolved into a cultural production and education initiative. We are applying for this grant to help create fertile ground for this endeavor, which will serve, in time, as a model that prioritizes economic and professional stability for artists and cultural managers.
