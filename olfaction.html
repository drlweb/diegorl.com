About the Project

During my time as an undergraduate research assistant in the Fleischmann lab, I designed a basic olfactory learning task by pairing odors with appetitive or aversive stimuli. Used doxycycline controlled expression of a fluorescent reporter for cFos in transgenic mice. Performed iDISCO tissue clearing and light-sheet microscopy to identify connectivity patterns of fos-tagged cells in volumetric images, and performed single-cell transcriptomics to identify genetic identity of fos-tagged cells using a data analysis pipeline in R integrating Seurat, Bioconductor and UMAP based Harmony algorithm.
